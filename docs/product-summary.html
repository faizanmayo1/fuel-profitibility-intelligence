What Fuel does

Real-time profitability intelligence for accounting firms

Fuel transforms accounting firms from reactive monthly reporters into proactive real-time CFO advisors. It unifies bookkeeping, payroll, GST, bank feeds, invoicing, and expense data into a single live financial model for every client — with no manual reconciliation cycles. The AI layer continuously detects margin leakage, cash-flow stress, payroll drift, and tax exposure, then generates ranked next-best actions for advisors to act on. Auto-written P&L narratives, BAS packs, and client emails replace static month-end reports with continuous financial storytelling. Built natively for AU/NZ firms with Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, KeyPay, NetPayroll, and ATO BAS integrations, Fuel lets advisory firms scale CFO-grade service across hundreds of clients without adding headcount.

The product · 12 screens

What each screen does

01
Advisor Dashboard / Demo hero

The advisor’s morning landing page. Surfaces a ranked priority queue of clients needing attention today, cross-portfolio AI signals, today’s advisory actions, and realised uplift — so the team knows who to call first before opening any other tab.

02
Client Portfolio /clients

Full searchable book of all 142 clients with live health scores, segments, top alerts, and integration sources. Live filters by segment, industry, health band, and advisor — one click drills into any client’s workspace.

03
Client Workspace /client/:slug Demo hero

Single-client deep dive. Live financial ledger, composite health score with driver breakdown, 42-day cash projection with shortfall window, AR aging by bucket, AI root-cause panel, recommended actions, and the live sync feed proving no manual reconciliation.

04
Profitability Leaks /profitability

Portfolio-wide margin leak detection grouped by root cause — pricing, cost inflation, labor inefficiency, or cash-timing mismatch. Each leak is service-line attributed, confidence-scored, and benchmarked against AU/NZ industry baselines.

05
Cash-flow Risk /cashflow

Predictive 30 / 60 / 90-day cash stress board with a runway leaderboard ranking clients by days-to-zero. Live scenario simulator visualises “invoices delayed 10 days” and “payroll +15%” on the cash trajectory to pre-empt shortfalls.

06
Payroll & Expense /payroll

Detects payroll efficiency drift and expense leakage. Tracks labor-ratio versus benchmark, payroll-vs-revenue index, overtime spikes, vendor concentration, subscription waste, and duplicate-bill anomalies across the book.

07
Tax & GST Risk /tax

Quarterly BAS readiness across the entire book. Shows predicted GST liability versus current tax reserve per client, shortfall projection, and AI-surfaced missed deductions backed against the ATO ruling library.

08
Advisory Recommendations /advisory

AI-ranked Next Best Action queue for the whole portfolio. Recommendations span pricing, cost reduction, payroll, tax efficiency, and cash management — with a Profit First allocation simulator and a realised-uplift acceptance funnel.

09
Auto Reporting /reports

Continuous financial reporting. AI writes monthly P&L narratives, BAS packs, board packs, and cash-flow commentary in plain English — with variance explanations tied to drivers, ready for advisor review and PDF export to the client.

10
Client Comms /comms

AI-drafted client emails and monthly advisory summaries, triggered automatically by alerts and recommendations. Four tone modes (Plain, Warm, Direct, Formal) tuned per client so the message lands the way they prefer to be spoken to.

11
Firm Operations /firm

Firm-wide advisor workload, SLA tracking, performance leaderboard, and client segmentation. A one-click Auto-assign flow rebalances high-risk clients across advisors based on expertise, capacity, and SLA window.

12
Integrations & Data Architecture /integrations

The platform layer. 10 connected sources with sync health and event volume, a live event stream showing every inbound transaction and its downstream recalculation, throughput chart, multi-tenant compliance posture, and an immutable audit log.
